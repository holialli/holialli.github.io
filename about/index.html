About

I'm Ali, a cybersecurity student at FAST-NUCES Islamabad. I mostly work on the defensive side: securing AWS setups, building CI/CD pipelines that won't ship a leaked secret or a vulnerable dependency, and writing detection and forensics tooling.

Most of my projects started as a university assignment or something I wanted to understand properly, and then I kept going. I try to write down what I got wrong as well as what worked, which is what the writing section is for.

How I build

  • Read-only by default. Scanners and audit tools shouldn't be able to change what they audit. When a fix is automated, it's opt-in and named explicitly.
  • Say what wasn't checked. A tool that can't see something should report that, not report a clean result.
  • Test the quiet case. A detection has to stay silent on normal activity, not just fire on the attack.
  • Keep evidence local. Forensic data stays on the examiner's machine unless they choose otherwise.
